## 2. Are 100 MB and 5 GB sensible?

### Receipts per cap

| File | Size | Free 100 MB | Pro 5 GB | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| App-prepared photo, bench average | 195 KB | ~510 | ~25,600 | 2026-11-07 bench, 31 receipts, `bytesSent` ([plan-limits review](2026-11-14-plan-limits-review.md)) |
| Same, p95 | 452 KB | ~220 | ~11,000 | same |
| Phone photo at the Standard target | 700 KiB (717 KB) | ~140 | ~7,000 | `ENCODING.targetBytes.recommended`, `apps/web/lib/upload-encoding.ts` |
| Photo at the hard cap | 1.5 MiB | ~63 | ~3,200 | `ENCODING.hardCapBytes` |
| PDF at the hosted upload limit | 4 MB | 25 | 1,250 | plan-limits review (Vercel body limit) |
| PDF at `MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES` | 10 MiB | ~9 | ~470 | `packages/core/src/upload-limits.ts` |

- The 195 KB average comes from bench fixtures (CORD, Wikimedia, web and generated receipts, six real Nepal bills),
  many of them small images. A 12 MP phone capture is encoded down a ladder toward the 700 KiB Standard target, so
  real phone uploads are likely 300 to 700 KB (**guess**; no production size data exists yet).
- **Time to fill Free at full use (50 a month):** about 10 months at 195 KB, about 3 months at 700 KB. A workspace that
  uploads PDF invoices from suppliers can fill it in under a month.
- **Pro at full use (500 a month):** 14 months at 700 KB, about 4 years at 195 KB. Most Pro workspaces will use far less
  than 500. 5 GB is fine.
- The plan-limits review measured the same numbers and recommended Free 1 GB and Pro 10 GB, deferred until the bucket
  passes 5 GB. The owner chose 100 MB and 5 GB.

### Cost: storage is not what the cap protects

R2 Standard is $0.015 per GB-month, 10 GB a month free ([Cloudflare R2 pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/),
2026-11-17). A full Free workspace costs $0.0015 a month, a full Pro one $0.075 (under 1% of the $8.05 left after
Paddle). Free at 1 GB would cost $0.015 per full workspace a month. The cap is a conversion lever and an abuse bound,
not a cost control. The scan limits already bound abuse: 50 × 10 MiB is about 0.5 GB a month at most.

- Of the receipt tools checked, only Dext's add-on is as small as 100 MB. The closest direct comparison, Zoho
  Expense's Free plan, which is sold in South Asia, includes 5 GB, 50 times ours.
- Competitors that state anything about keeping receipts state **retention in years**, not bytes. Customers think "will
  my receipts still be there at tax time", not "how many MB". Indian billing tools (myBillBook, Vyapar) publish no
  storage terms in what was found (third-party comparisons only, 2026).

### Is storage worth showing on pricing?

Yes, because it is enforced: hiding a hard limit is worse than showing a small one, and decision 2026-11-14 says the
cards list what is enforced. But:

1. **The app shows no storage.** On Billing, the plan cards list only scans and members, and the line under the meter
   reads `50 a month · 20 a day · 2 members` (screenshot). Decision 2026-11-14 says storage is shown "always on
   Billing". Both PRs defer it to a follow-up (#291 "Left for after #290"). Merge and enable them together, or a user
   reaches a limit they couldn't see.
2. **Set Free where it won't bite before Pro can be bought.** While checkout is closed, a full Free workspace can only
   delete receipts. My recommendation is Free 500 MB (about 700 to 2,500 photos, 14+ months of full use at 700 KB;
   $0.0075 a month per full workspace), Pro unchanged. It's an Admin → Settings value. Decide before the first user
   reaches the cap, because the no-dated-offers rule makes lowering it later awkward.
3. **Re-measure in two weeks.** After the backfill, `avg(file_bytes)` per receipt and per workspace replaces the
   guesses above. If the average phone photo is under 250 KB, 100 MB is defensible.

## 4. Build order

The handoff order is: storage cap → Settings → Usage page → line editing → VAT purchase book.

- **Land all four PRs.** Make the **storage row on Billing and the card's storage-full state** part of the same
  release as #291's enforcement, not a later one.
- **Move the Settings → Usage page after the VAT export.** Its new content is per-member breakdowns and scans per day.
  These serve teams we don't have yet. A solo user's needs are covered by the card and Billing. Saves about 1 to 2 days
  before line editing.
- **Line editing next is right.** Weighed items rounding to whole numbers is a correctness bug users see on every
  grocery or fuel receipt ("Line items can't be edited yet" is on the receipt page). Reviewed receipts feed the VAT
  export by default, so editing is a prerequisite.
- **VAT export after that, but only once D1 is answered.** It is the best reason a Nepal business would choose us over
  Zoho or a spreadsheet. A Nepal business can't pay through Paddle easily (11-13 F2). If Nepal is first, an NPR price
  with Khalti or eSewa, or a manual bank transfer, has to come before the VAT export ships. If international cards come
  first, the VAT export can wait behind R3 and the hosting and backup work.
- **Nothing in these four should be cut.** #279 is required by the privacy policy, and #283 is a security review item
  done at no user-visible cost (about 250 ms per sign-in).
