stranger pay: a cheap default, honest copy with a price, a checkout, and a review loop that closes.

## 3. Pricing, packaging, default model

**Model prices (fetched 2026-11-07):** Haiku 5.5 $0.10 in / $0.50 out; Sonnet 5.5 $2 / $10; Opus 5.5 $4 / $20
([platform.claude.com pricing](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing)). Gemini 3.8 Flash $0.75 /

([aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing)). `packages/ai/src/models.ts` matches.

failure" step, not as step one.

contact us for bespoke pricing" ([paddle.com/pricing](https://www.paddle.com/pricing)). So **Pro $10/mo or $90/yr**
(annual fee 5.6%; lead with annual), **500 receipts/month pooled per workspace**, Free **25–30/month**. Anchors
today: SparkReceipt Pro $6.90/mo for 50 docs, Elite $12.90 unlimited, no free plan

**BYOK:** a Pro perk, never a tier or a landing claim (byok.md §1.3 agrees). I'd revisit the owner's Free own-key

## 5. Risks

- **Building without a buyer.** Zero paying users, zero interviews, ~12 open plans. Five calls with cross-border
  freelancers (11-06 market review) are still the cheapest test.
- **Provider.** Three providers cover single-provider risk; the new gap is **Gemini 3.8 Flash has no benchmark result**
  (free-tier key, 503s) yet sits second in the chain. Use Flash-Lite, which ran.
- **Data and privacy.** US processors, retention "until you delete", ledger kept for ever (STATUS decision 10). Fine
  for freelancers; a DPA blocker for EU bookkeepers. Don't sell to them yet.
- **Support load.** One person, contact form only. BYOK, Google Cloud billing and Khalti registration each add
  tickets; review state and the feedback inbox remove them.
- **Payment approval.** MoRs reject thin "AI wrapper" sites (**guess**, from payments.md). A personal subdomain, a
  generic name and a fake-domain hero don't help; items 2–3 double as the approval case.
- **January price shock**: Gemini Flash doubles. Haiku then Flash-Lite avoids it.
