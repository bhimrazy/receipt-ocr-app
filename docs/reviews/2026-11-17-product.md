## Verdict

#283 and #279 are owner-ready from the product side: password sign-in, a wrong password, the reset flow and the
legacy-hash upgrade all behave, and the privacy page states every new retention period in plain words. #290 does what
the accepted design says in every state I could build (near, day used up, month used up, opens soon), on the card and
on the phone strip. The set is not owner-ready because of #291: the storage cap is enforced and advertised on
/pricing, but nothing in the app shows it until an upload is refused. When it is refused, the upload panel tells the
user to wait for a reset that never comes.

## Findings

### Owner-blocking

**1. A full or nearly full workspace gets no warning anywhere in the app, and the in-app plan cards omit storage (#291 with #290).**
- Steps: set a Free workspace to 85 MB, then 99.99 MB, of 100 MB. Open Home, Add receipts and Plan and billing at 1440 and 390.
- Expected (owner's accepted design and `docs/product-standards.md` "A usage warning shows from 80% used on the pages
  where scanning starts, at every width"): a storage row on the card from 80%, and storage on Billing at all times.
  Billing's plan cards list what /pricing lists.
- Actual: no mention of storage on any app page at 85% or at 99.99%. Plan and billing shows `50 a month · 20 a day ·
  2 members` and Free/Pro cards with scans and members only. /pricing says "100 MB of receipt storage" and "5 GB".
  /terms "Plans and limits" doesn't mention storage either. The upgrade modal's comparison has no storage row.
- Screenshots: `S/product/shots/stor85-app@390-dark.png`, `stor85-app@1440-dark.png`,
  `storfull-app-upload@390-light.png`, `storfull-app-settings-billing@390-dark.png`, `stor85-app-settings-billing@1440-dark.png`;
  compare `S/shots/public-dark/pricing@1440-dark.png`.
- Fix: land #290's deferred storage rows (card from 80%, Billing always, `… · 100 MB storage` in the limits line,
  `storageLine` on both Billing plan cards) and the terms sentence with `LEGAL_UPDATED.terms`, in the same release as
  enforcement. If they can't land together, hold #291's enforcement behind them rather than ship a limit users can't see.

**2. The storage refusal tells the user to wait for a reset (#291).**
- Steps: workspace at 100 MB (or 99.99 MB). Add receipts, choose two files, Upload.
- Expected: one instruction: delete receipts to make room. No reset, because storage doesn't reset.
- Actual: the banner reads "This workspace's storage is full: 100 MB used. Delete receipts to make room. **The rest are
  still here; scan them when your limit resets.**" Under the queue: "38 left this month. Resets 1 December". "Retry" on
  each row and "Scan 2 receipts" stay active, and each press sends the upload again and is refused again (5 refusals
  in a row in the walk).
- Cause: the suffix is hard-coded for every stop reason at `apps/web/components/upload-panel.tsx:697`.
- Screenshots: `S/product/shots/up-full-2@390-light.png`, `S/product/shots/up-nofit-2@1440-dark.png`.
- Fix: choose the suffix by `kind`. For storage: "The rest are still here; delete receipts to make room, then scan
  them." Hide per-row Retry for a storage refusal, or disable Scan until something is deleted.

### Polish

**3. "100 MB of 100 MB used" while the message says the file doesn't fit (#291).** At 99.99 MB the refusal says "This
file doesn't fit in this workspace's storage: 100 MB of 100 MB used." `formatStorage` rounds
(`packages/core/src/plans.ts:86`), so a reader sees a full workspace described as not full. Fix: round used space down
(`Math.floor`) in refusals and meters, so 99.99 reads "99 MB of 100 MB". The same applies at 4.96 GB of 5 GB, and
999.6 MB shows as "1000 MB". Screenshot: `S/product/shots/up-nofit-2@1440-dark.png`.

**4. Two units for the same thing (#291).** Upload hints say "PDFs up to 4.0 MB" in binary MiB with a decimal
(`apps/web/components/upload-panel.tsx:65`), while storage uses decimal whole MB ("100 MB"). Fix: use `formatStorage`,
or one rule for file sizes everywhere.

**5. The pricing FAQ "What happens when I reach a limit?" says "Uploads pause until the limit resets" (#291).** True for
scans but not for storage. Fix: add "Storage doesn't reset: delete receipts to make room." (`apps/web/app/pricing/page.tsx:108-109`).

**6. The privacy page states the photo re-encoding fact twice, differently (#279).** "What we collect" now says
"your browser re-encodes **most** photos … a PDF, or a photo it can't re-encode, keeps whatever is embedded". "Who
processes your data" still says "Photos **are** re-encoded in your browser before upload … PDFs, and HEIC files your
browser can't convert, are stored as uploaded." (`apps/web/app/privacy/page.tsx:75`). Fix: cut that sentence from line
75, leaving the provider list, so the page makes the claim once.

**7. Privacy says background-task records are deleted after 30/90 days (#279 with #291).** The first
`receipt.file_size_backfill` job (and the `receipt.recheck` one) is kept indefinitely by design
(`packages/core/src/jobs/retention.ts:18`). Neither holds personal data, so this is accuracy, not risk. Fix: "Records of
background tasks **about you or your messages**, such as…", or name the exception.

**8. Reset date written two ways on one screen (#290).** The card says "10 left · resets 1 Dec" and "Limit reached ·
opens 1 Dec". The meter on the same Billing page says "Resets 1 December, 00:00 UTC". The month-up page says "1
December, in 14 days". Fix: "1 December" on the card too, if it fits at 256 px. Otherwise keep "1 Dec" and note it as
the short form. Screenshot: `S/product/shots/near-app-settings-billing@1440-dark.png`.

**9. "Compare plans" in the opens-soon note has no 44 px target on phones (#290).** The Upgrade and Contact variants get
`max-md:min-h-11`, the opens-soon `Link` doesn't (`apps/web/components/upgrade/meter-action-view.tsx:61-63`). On the
44 px strip it is a 16 px tall text link (WCAG 2.5.5). Screenshot: `S/product/shots/near-app-upload@390-light.png`.

**10. Per-row refusal is truncated at 390 (#291).** "This workspace's storage …" cuts off before the action. The banner
above carries it, so this is minor. Fix: let the row error wrap to two lines. Screenshot: `S/product/shots/up-full-2@390-light.png`.

### Ideas

## What was checked and passed

- **#283 password sign-in.** Correct password signs in. A wrong password shows "That email and password don't match an
  account. Check both and try again." inline with an icon, at 390 and 1440 (`signin-wrong@390-light.png`,
  `signin-wrong@1440-dark.png`). Reset: "Check your email … expires in 1 hour" → "Choose a new password … signed out on
  every other device" → `/sign-in?reset=1` with "Password updated. Sign in with your new password." → signs in with
  the new password (`reset-form`, `reset-done`, `reset-signedin@390-light.png`). The stored hash is `$scrypt$N=32768,r=8,p=3`.
  A Better Auth legacy hash refused a wrong password (401), accepted the right one (200) and was rewritten to the new
  format. The emailed link could not be seen, because a production build without a mailer doesn't log it. I followed
  Better Auth's own `/api/auth/reset-password/<token>` redirect with a token seeded in `verification`.
- **#290 states.** Near month (40/50): amber bar, "10 left · resets 1 Dec", "Pro, with 500 scans a month, opens soon.
  Compare plans", and the phone strip on Home and Add receipts at 390 and nowhere else. Day used up (20 today, 40 month):
  "Daily limit reached … in 19 hours. 10 of 50 are left this month." Month used up: upload input removed, "Uploads open
  again on 1 December, in 14 days". No "Upgrade" anywhere while checkout is closed (standards line 21). At 2/50 the
  phone shows no strip and nothing pitches Pro (standards line 33).
- **#291 enforcement.** A 412-byte PDF fits at 99.99 MB. A larger photo is refused with the "doesn't fit" copy. At
  exactly 100 MB, both files are refused with the "is full" copy, a 429 with no `Retry-After`. No copy says "upgrade".
- **#279 privacy page.** Every new period is stated (sessions, step-up records 90 days, invitations 30 days after
  expiry, task records 30/90 days, admin log 2 years) in plain words with no idioms.
- **Standing rules.** No launch dates, early access or grandfathering in any of the four PRs ("2 months free" on
  yearly is a permanent price, not a dated offer). The storage numbers on /pricing are enforced by working code
  (`ingestUpload` checks before and under the workspace lock). They are not shown in-app (finding 1).
- **Tester feedback 2026-11-13.** Not touched by these PRs, and still in place. Saving an edit showed "Reviewed by
  Riley Reset · Undo" (`edit-saved@390-dark.png`). Help is rows ("Help and feedback" in Settings and the account menu,
  "Report a problem" in the receipt menu, no floating launcher in `/app`). Merchant grouping goes through
  `packages/core/src/merchant-key.ts`. Line warnings were not re-walked.

## Proposed product standards

- "A limit that never resets (storage) is never described with reset wording. Its refusal and its warning say only
  how to make room." (2026-11-17)
- "A limit is enforced only once it is shown: the app's meter and Billing, the plan cards on /pricing and Billing, and
  /terms all list it in the same release." (2026-11-17)
- "Used amounts are rounded down, so a limit never reads as reached before it is." (2026-11-17)
