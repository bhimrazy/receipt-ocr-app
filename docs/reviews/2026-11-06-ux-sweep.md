## Verdict

The signed-in app is fast and mostly well-behaved. Two things would have drawn an owner correction: (1) the landing page
still made every claim the 11-04 and 11-06 reviews asked to remove, and (2) two pages scrolled sideways at 390 once an
error or invite link appeared. Both are fixed on this branch, along with copy that contradicted the usage ledger and
photo-only wording on failed PDFs.

## Proposed product-standards lines

- Copy names what the person uploaded: "PDF" for a PDF, never "photo". (2026-11-06)
- A quota rule is worded the same way everywhere it appears and matches what the ledger counts. (2026-11-06)
- The 390 check includes error, success and long-content states (long file names, invite links, emails), not only the
  empty page. (2026-11-06)
- Counts in copy agree with their number ("1 receipt", "2 receipts"). (2026-11-06)
