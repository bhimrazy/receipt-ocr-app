# Market research: Receipt OCR (2026-11-06)

_Adds to `scratchpad/reviews/2026-11-06-ceo.md` (today) and `receipt-ocr-backend/docs/reviews/2026-11-04-market.md`.
It does not repeat their model-cost table, the claims sweep, or the table-stakes gap list. All vendor pages were fetched
2026-11-06 unless marked. **(3P)** = third-party source only. **(conflict)** = sources disagree. Vendor pages were
read through a summarizer, so check any number before it goes on our site._

## Verdict

1. **Pick freelancers and sole traders who hand their books to an accountant, with messy, foreign and multi-currency
   receipts.** Do not pick developers first (the 11-04 review said developers first). The API price floor has collapsed:
   AWS and Google charge **$0.01/page**, Mistral about **$0.005**, and any developer can call Gemini directly for less
   than our cost-plus price. Freelancer apps sell at **$5–13/month** with small allowances. We can match that band and
   give 5–10x the receipts.
2. **Our current free tier (20/day) is not a differentiator.** A no-signup web tool, CleanTably, already gives
   **20 receipts/day with no account** **(3P)**. Accounting suites include capture at no extra cost: Xero has Smart
   Document Capture on every plan, and Wave Pro has unlimited receipts. **Free capture is now a given. The paid
   product is a clean, accountant-ready ledger.**
3. **Defensible differentiators (2–3):** (a) line items, tax and category on every plan, including foreign and
   multi-currency receipts (incumbents charge extra for line items); (b) review speed plus accountant-ready export with
   per-currency totals; (c) a high receipt allowance per dollar, which our cost makes possible. Privacy, speed and API
   access do not count: they are claims anyone can make, or things we have not built.
4. **Distribution:** a no-signup "receipt to Excel/CSV" tool as the lead magnet; programmatic pages by country/tax
   regime and by "X alternative"; communities. Defer the Xero and QuickBooks marketplaces. Xero certification needs live
   connections, and Intuit's program is US-based.

**What the table shows:**

- **Price per receipt, end-user tools:** Shoeboxed $0.18 ($9/50), SparkReceipt $0.14 ($6.90/50), Dext $0.10 (+$0.50 for line
  items), Receiptor $0.58 ($29/50), Lido $0.29 ($29/100), Foreceipt $0.025 ($5/200). Our cost is $0.0013–0.026
  (CEO review §4). **Small allowances at $5–13 are the norm**, so a generous allowance is the gap we can fill.
- **Line items are an upsell everywhere:** Dext charges $0.50 per document, and Zoho limits itemized OCR to paid
  plans. Itemizing a receipt costs an LLM almost nothing extra.
- **Free tiers are tiny or missing:** none at SparkReceipt and Shoeboxed; 100 lifetime at Foreceipt; 20/user/mo at Zoho;
  50 pages lifetime at Lido. The exception is no-signup web tools at 20/day.
- **SparkReceipt is the closest model to copy and beat:** the plan is shared by the account, extra users cost $1, the
  accountant joins free, and it has QBO/Xero/DATEV templates. It has **no free plan**.

## 2. Segments and jobs-to-be-done

| Segment | Job | Compares against | Reachable for us? | Willing to pay? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Freelancers / sole traders** | "Turn this month's receipts into rows my accountant (or tax return) accepts, without typing them" | Spreadsheet + manual entry (vendor estimates of 2–4 h/mo, up to 12 h, "3 min per expense", all unsourced **(3P)**) ([Discury](https://discury.io/report/best-expense-tracking-apps-for-freelancers-reddit), [SparkReceipt blog](https://sparkreceipt.com/blog/expense-tracking-freelancers)); free tools; the capture in their accounting suite | **High:** self-serve, search-driven, communities | **Yes, $5–13/mo** (SparkReceipt, Shoeboxed, Foreceipt, Smart Receipts all sell here) |

| **Bookkeepers / accountants** | Process many clients' documents into the ledger | Dext (~$18/client **(3P)**), Hubdoc ($12, bundled with Xero), AutoEntry | Medium-low: needs direct sync, client portals, trust; slow sales | Yes, per client, but switching cost is high |
| **Developers** | Receipt image to JSON inside their own app | AWS/Google/Azure at $0.01, Mistral at ~$0.005, Gemini called directly | High reach (HN, docs SEO) | **Low margin:** the price floor is near our cost; Veryfi at $0.08 sells to enterprise on SLAs, not price |

No independent survey of spreadsheet use by freelancers was found. The only survey number is CPA.com 2023: 57% of
SMBs "spend too much time on expense management" (cited secondhand) ([Harvest](https://www.getharvest.com/expenses/freelance-expense-tracker)).
Treat "spreadsheet" as the competitor in copy, not as a measured figure.

**Pick: freelancers and sole traders, especially cross-border ones** (contractors paid in one currency who spend in
others, digital nomads, small ecommerce sellers). Reasons: they are reachable through search and communities; they
already pay in our price band; their pain grows with receipts we read well (foreign languages, mixed currencies, long
itemized bills); and the accountant they hand off to brings in the bookkeeper segment later. The "Accountant joins
free" and per-currency totals features serve both. Keep the API as a secondary channel that reuses `packages/core`,
not as the wedge.

## 3. Differentiation: what is credible and defensible

| Option | Credible now? | Defensible? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per receipt / generous allowance | Yes. Our cost is 10–100x below competitors' per-receipt price | Weak alone (any LLM entrant can match it), but incumbents with OCR cost structures and $0.50 line-item add-ons can't follow without cutting their prices | **Pick, as "more receipts per dollar"**, not as "cheapest" |
| Accuracy on messy / foreign / handwritten receipts, line items included | Only after the benchmark runs. Evidence: a 2026 vendor study found ~97% key-value accuracy for an LLM ([AIMultiple](https://research.aimultiple.com/receipt-ocr), Claude 3.5, custom set); a self-published SROIE/CORD test found an LLM step lifts field F1 from 8–34% to 37–62% ([imagetotable](https://imagetotable.ai/de/references/regex-vs-llm-field-extraction)). Taggun claims "90%+" | Medium: a **public benchmark on hard receipts** (non-English, faded, multi-currency, long) is content others must answer | **Pick**, gated on the benchmark |
| Multi-currency done right (per-currency totals, home-currency view, never summing across currencies) | Close: the currency field exists; per-currency totals are CEO roadmap week 5 | Medium: a workflow detail incumbents treat as an afterthought | **Pick**, bundled with accountant export |
| Accountant-ready export (QBO/Xero/Excel templates, "accountant joins free") | After CEO roadmap week 5 | Low–medium (SparkReceipt has it), but it is needed to compete | Table stakes for the segment |
| Review speed (image beside fields, low-confidence queue) | After CEO roadmap week 4 | Medium: the UX compounds; it is the real time saved | Supports the accuracy story |
| Speed (seconds per receipt) | Plausible | None: Taggun says "a few seconds" | Don't lead with it |
| Privacy / data residency | No. Receipts go to Google; no EU-region model or retention controls | Would need an EU-hosted model and a DPA | Not now. Revisit for EU bookkeepers |
| API access | Not built | None at a $0.005–0.01 floor | Secondary channel |
| Bulk upload | Adequate (20 files) | None | Table stakes |
| Email-in | Not built (roadmap week 6) | None (Hubdoc, Receiptor, SparkReceipt have it) | Table stakes for retention |

**The three to stand on:** (1) line items, tax and category included on every receipt, any language; (2)
multi-currency books your accountant can import; (3) a large monthly allowance at the price of a small one. Each one
answers a gap in a priced competitor offer above (Dext's $0.50 line-item fee, Zoho's itemized paywall, SparkReceipt's
50 docs for $6.90).

## 5. Pricing recommendation

**Unit: receipts per month, pooled per workspace.** One PDF of up to 5 pages counts as one receipt. Failed reads don't
count (already true). This answers CEO decision #2: the plan belongs to the **workspace**, not the user, as
SparkReceipt does ("50/month shared by the account", $1 per extra user). Per-user quotas reward creating extra members
to get more free receipts. Per-seat pricing is the model of reimbursement tools (Expensify, Zoho), which we are not.

| Plan | Price | Allowance | Members | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0, no card | **25 receipts/mo** + all features, including line items and CSV/XLSX export | 1 + accountant (read-only) | Beats Zoho's 20/user, SparkReceipt's none and Foreceipt's 100 lifetime. Export is the "aha", so don't gate it. ~$0.20–0.65/mo at the 2027 Flash rate |
| **Pro** | **$9/mo or $90/yr** | **500/mo** | 2 + accountant free | Agrees with the CEO review. $0.018/receipt vs SparkReceipt $0.14, Shoeboxed $0.18. Worst case $13 at the heavy 2027 rate (CEO §4), so the benchmark must confirm a cheaper default model, or cap at 300 |
| **Team** | **$29/mo or $290/yr** | **2,500/mo pooled** | 5, then $3/member | Matches the CEO "Business" tier. Undercuts Dext ($25.21 for 250 docs, line items extra) on allowance |
| **Top-up** | $5 per 250 receipts ($0.02 each), no expiry | n/a | n/a | Replaces "unlimited". Matches SparkReceipt's never-expiring credit packs |
| **API** (later) | 50 free/mo, then $0.02/receipt, no minimum | n/a | n/a | Only once keys and docs exist. 2x Google/AWS, a quarter of Veryfi; buyers pay for line items + category + currency, not raw OCR |

Not recommended: daily caps (CEO §4); a Solo tier under $9 (the 11-04 review's $6/300 splits a small market into two
tiers before there are any buyers); per-client accountant pricing before ~20 paying freelancers.

**Positioning statement.** _For freelancers and sole traders whose receipts come in many languages and currencies,
Receipt OCR turns photos and PDFs into accountant-ready rows (merchant, date, tax, every line item, category) that you
check in seconds. Unlike Dext or Zoho, line items are on every plan. Unlike SparkReceipt or Shoeboxed, you get 500
receipts for $9, not 50._ Every claim in it must be true and benchmarked before it goes live (product-standards). Drop
the "500 vs 50" comparison if Pro's cap changes.
