# Design review: usage card, storage cap, privacy retention, review statuses (2026-11-17)

Read-only critique of the visible changes in the open PRs #290, #291 and #279, plus #278's statuses and duplicate
chips as they look live. Follows [the 2026-11-14 usage and pricing review](2026-11-14-usage-and-pricing-design.md).

## Inputs

- Combined build (main plus #279, #290, #291), every public and app route at 1440 and 390 px, dark and light, in the
  review scratch directory `shots/` (`public-*`, `app-*`). The seeded account sits at 10 / 50 scans, so the combined
  build shows only the card's normal state.
- #290's PR screenshots (9 card states, 36 images), named below as `pr-290/<state>-<width>-<theme>.png`.
- #291's PR screenshots (`pr-291/pricing@…`, `pr-291/admin-settings@…`). The admin pages in `shots/admin-*` didn't
  capture: sign-in returned HTTP 429.
- #279's PR screenshots and the combined `privacy@…` captures.
- Code read on `review/2026-11-17` (`254c49a`) where a screenshot couldn't show a state: `lib/meter-action.ts`,
  `components/upgrade/*`, `components/upload-panel.tsx`, `lib/capture/batch-queue.ts`,
  `packages/core/src/storage-quota.ts`, `packages/core/src/plans.ts` (`formatStorage`), `app/privacy/page.tsx`.

Seeded receipts mostly show "Possible duplicate" because fixture OCR returns the same few receipts. That is test data,
and the findings below don't count it as a bug. Every 390 capture of #290 has the `next dev` badge ("N") over the Home
tab. It is a dev-server artifact, not a product defect.

Severity: **Must-fix** blocks merge or release. **Should-fix** goes in this PR or a tracked follow-up before launch.
**Nit** is optional polish.

## What works

- **The card reads month-first in every state.** The count is always the month, and the note states one fact. The
  first-run confusion from finding 1 of 11-14 ("0 / 50 · 20 left today") is gone. `daynear` shows
  `17 / 50 scans` with `(!) 3 left today` and no offer, as decision 4 says.
- **Amber is carried by words as well.** Every amber bar has an icon and a sentence. In light theme the warning token
  renders as a dark amber (`pr-290/near-1440-light.png`), which separates from the track far better than a yellow
  would.
- **One action rule.** `meterAction` drives both the card and the strip, and the member variants ("Ask the owner for
  Pro.", nothing for the pool) are correct in every capture. On phones `size="sm"` and the link variants grow to
  44 px (`max-md:h-11`, `max-md:min-h-11`).
- **Pro at 100% gets "Need more? Contact us"**, and Pro members get nothing (`pr-290/pro-1440-dark.png`).
- **The refusal copy keeps the standard.** It never says "upgrade", it states the limit and says what works now.
- **Pricing lists storage in the agreed row order** (scans, members, storage, workspaces), on all three widths and
  both themes (`pricing@1440-dark.png`, `pr-291/pricing@390-light.png`).
- **The admin storage row** says its unit, its conversion ("1 GB is 1000") and what happens to a workspace already
  over the cap.
- **The privacy additions are concrete:** each period is a number, with the reason stated ("to spot attempts to
  break in").

## Findings

### #290: usage card and phone strip

| #   | Severity   | Screen and state                                  | Screenshot                                                   |
| --- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| U1  | Must-fix   | Home, Free owner at 80% of the month, 390 and 1440 | `pr-290/near-390-dark.png`, `pr-290/near-1440-dark.png`      |
| U2  | Must-fix   | Card and strip, Free owner, checkout closed        | `pr-290/nonadmin-1440-dark.png`, `pr-290/nonadmin-390-*.png` |
| U3  | Should-fix | Home and Add receipts, day used up, limit card    | `pr-290/dayup-390-dark.png`, `pr-290/dayup-1440-light.png`   |
| U4  | Should-fix | Home, Pro owner at a used-up month, 390           | `pr-290/pro-390-dark.png`                                    |
| U5  | Nit        | Card notes across states                          | `pr-290/near-*`, `up-*`, `dayup-*`, `pool-*`                 |
| U6  | Nit        | Home card vs strip wording at 80%                 | `pr-290/near-390-dark.png`                                   |

**U1: two upgrade pitches for the same fact on Home.** At 390, the new strip
(`(!) 10 scans left this month · resets 1 Dec` / `Upgrade >`) sits above the drop zone. The existing
`UpgradeHomeCard` sits below the buttons with the same fact, a full-width primary `Upgrade to Pro` and `Not now`. At
1440 the sidebar link and the home card's primary button show together. Two asks on one screen at 80% contradict the
decision behind the strip: a link from 80%, the button only once the month is used up. The pitch also feels heavier
than the limit itself.
**Fix:** below `lg`, hide `UpgradeHomeCard` whenever the strip renders, so the strip is the one phone surface. At
`lg` and above, keep the home card. It is dismissible and the sidebar link is small, so the pair is acceptable there.
Owner decision D1 covers the alternative.

**U2: the "checkout closed" state isn't shown.** The `nonadmin-*` set is captioned "Free owner while checkout is
closed ('opens soon')". It shows `Upgrade >` on the card and `Upgrade to Pro` on the home card, with no
`Pro, with 500 scans a month, opens soon. Compare plans` anywhere. Either the captures are mislabelled (a
non-platform-admin owner with checkout open) or the card ignores closed checkout. The second would break the product
standard: "Upgrade" appears only beside a control that starts it. The code path exists (`opens-soon` in
`meterAction` when `audience` is null), but no capture proves it.
**Fix:** capture an owner at 40 / 50 with checkout closed at 1440 and 390. The card and the strip must read
`Pro, with 500 scans a month, opens soon. Compare plans`, and the home card must not render with an Upgrade button.
Relabel the current set as "Free owner, not a platform admin".

**U3: the day-limit card still leads with a button.** On a used-up day the sidebar card offers a link, but the limit
card in the upload panel offers a primary `Upgrade to Pro` above `Review receipts`. Decision 4 was a text link for the
day, because it resets within hours.
**Fix:** for `blocked === "day"`, make `Review receipts` the primary button. Replace the Pro line and button with one
line, `Pro allows up to 200 a day in this workspace. Upgrade`, where `Upgrade` is the same link as the card. The
used-up month keeps the button.

**U4: on a phone, a Pro owner at a used-up month has no way to contact us from Home.** The contact link exists only on
the card, which phones show in the account sheet. The limit card offers only `Review receipts`.
**Fix:** under the limit card's text, for a Pro owner only, add `Need more? Contact us`, using the same
`ContactButton` and the "use-case" topic.

**U5: one event, two verbs.** The card says `10 left · resets 1 Dec` at 80%, then `Limit reached · opens 1 Dec` at
100%, `Today's limit reached · opens 00:00 UTC` and `Free scanning busy · opens 00:00 UTC`. "Opens" has no subject on
a card about scans; that wording came from my 11-14 spec, and this review corrects it.
**Fix:**

| State                | Now                                     | New                                        |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Month used up        | `Limit reached · opens 1 Dec`           | `Limit reached · resets 1 Dec`             |
| Day used up          | `Today's limit reached · opens 00:00 UTC` | `Today's limit reached · resets 00:00 UTC` |
| Free pool full       | `Free scanning busy · opens 00:00 UTC`  | `Free scanning busy · back at 00:00 UTC`   |
| Paused for everyone  | `Paused for everyone · opens 00:00 UTC` | `Paused for everyone · back at 00:00 UTC`  |

**U6: "free scans" on one surface, "scans" on the other.** The home card's title is `10 free scans left this month`;
the strip and the card say `10 scans left this month` / `10 left`.
**Fix:** use `10 scans left this month` in `homeCardCopy`. The badge already says Free.

Also note: the Billing limits line still reads `50 a month · 20 a day · 2 members`. The 11-14 spec adds
` · 100 MB storage`. That waits for the storage follow-up (S2).

### #291: storage cap

| #   | Severity   | Screen and state                                             | Screenshot                                                            |
| --- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| S1  | Must-fix   | Add receipts, batch stopped by a storage refusal             | none; code, `upload-panel.tsx` (stopped alert)                        |
| S2  | Must-fix (release) | Billing, sidebar card, upload panel; storage near or at the cap | `app-settings-billing@1440-dark.png`, `app-settings-billing@390-light.png` |
| S3  | Should-fix | Refusal text, the "doesn't fit" variant                      | none; code, `storageMessage` and `formatStorage`                      |
| S4  | Should-fix | Billing plan cards (in app)                                  | `app-settings-billing@1440-dark.png`                                  |
| S5  | Should-fix | Add receipts, one large file in a batch doesn't fit          | none; code, `batch-queue.ts`                                          |
| S6  | Nit        | Refusal text, the "full" variant                             | none                                                                  |
| S7  | Nit        | Admin → Settings, "Storage per workspace (MB)"               | `pr-291/admin-settings@1440-dark.png`                                 |

**S1: the storage refusal promises a reset that never happens.** When the queue stops, the panel renders
`{snapshot.stopped} The rest are still here; scan them when your limit resets.` For storage the reader gets:

> This workspace's storage is full: 100 MB used. Delete receipts to make room. The rest are still here; scan them
> when your limit resets.

Storage doesn't reset, so the last sentence is false and contradicts the one before it.
**Fix:** choose the follow-on sentence by refusal kind. For `kind: "storage"`, use
`The rest are still in the list; add them once there's room.` Keep today's sentence for day and month limits. Add a
test that the storage text never contains "resets".

**S2: the cap is enforced but invisible until it refuses.** In the combined build, storage appears only on the public
pricing page and in the refusal. Billing has no storage meter, the card has no storage row, and the upload panel has
no full state. So someone at 100 MB picks and crops ten photos, then is refused on upload. #291 leaves these rows to a
follow-up after #290. That order is fine for merging, but not for a production release.
**Fix:** release #291's enforcement to production only with the follow-up (or fold it in). It needs:

- Billing: an always-shown storage meter, `86 MB of 100 MB used.`, adding `Delete receipts to make room.` from 80%.
- The card: a storage row from 80%.
- The upload panel: a storage-full state that replaces the drop zone, like the limit card:
  - title `This workspace's storage is full`;
  - body `It has used all 100 MB. Delete receipts you no longer need to make room. Deleting doesn't give scans back.`;
  - buttons `Review receipts` (primary) and, for a Free owner with checkout open, an `Upgrade` link;
  - for a member, `Ask the owner for Pro.`

  The 11-14 spec's state tables have the rest.

**S3: rounding makes "doesn't fit" read as full.** `formatStorage` rounds to the nearest whole MB. At 99.6 MB used, a
4 MB file gets `This file doesn't fit in this workspace's storage: 100 MB of 100 MB used.`, which looks full. A Pro
workspace at 4.96 GB reads `5 GB of 5 GB used`. Under 0.5 MB used reads `0 MB`, where the spec said `<1 MB`. The
message also never gives the file's size, so the reader can't judge how much to delete.
**Fix:** round used space down when displaying it, show `<1 MB` below 1 MB, and state what's left:
`This file (4 MB) doesn't fit: this workspace has 1 MB of its 100 MB storage left. Delete receipts to make room.`
Under 10 MB left, show one decimal (`0.4 MB`).

**S4: the in-app plan cards omit storage.** Pricing lists scans, members, storage and workspaces. Billing's Free and
Pro cards list only scans and members (`plan-and-billing.tsx` passes `[scansLine, membersLine]`). The PR body says
"The plan cards list storage". One product, two different lists.
**Fix:** Billing's cards use the same `freeFeatures`/`storageLine` rows as pricing, in the same order. At minimum add
`100 MB of receipt storage` / `5 GB of receipt storage` after members.

**S5: one file that doesn't fit stops the whole batch.** The storage 429 has no `Retry-After`, so `batch-queue` stops.
That is right when storage is full. In the "doesn't fit" case, smaller files in the same batch could still go in.
**Fix (owner decision D3):** for the not-full variant, fail only that file (its row shows the message) and continue
the batch. Stop the batch only when the response says storage is full.

**S6: "100 MB used" restates the limit.** `This workspace's storage is full: 100 MB used.` reads as a measurement,
not a limit.
**Fix:** `This workspace's 100 MB of storage is full. Delete receipts to make room.`

**S7: the admin row doesn't say whether it can be empty.** Every other row with a meaning for empty says so
("Empty means no monthly limit").
**Fix:** add `Empty means no limit.`, or make the field required and say `Required.` As a separate polish, add a
computed hint under a value of 1000 or more (`5000 MB = 5 GB`).

**To verify before release:** can a member delete receipts other people uploaded? If not, "Delete receipts to make
room" asks a member for something they can't do. The member copy should then be
`This workspace's 100 MB of storage is full. Ask the owner to make room.`

### #279: privacy page

| #   | Severity   | Screen and state                                       | Screenshot                                                  |
| --- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| P1  | Should-fix | What we collect → Receipts, and Who processes → Hosting | `privacy@1440-dark.png`, `pr-279/privacy@390-light.png`     |
| P2  | Should-fix | Sign-in security vs Sign-in records                    | `privacy@1440-dark.png`                                     |
| P3  | Should-fix | How long we keep it, 390                               | `privacy@390-dark.png`, `privacy@390-light.png`             |
| P4  | Should-fix | Admin log sentence                                     | `privacy@1440-dark.png`                                     |
| P5  | Nit        | Header date                                            | `privacy@1440-dark.png`                                     |

**P1: the re-encoding fact appears twice, in two versions.** The new Receipts sentence says "your browser re-encodes
*most* photos … a PDF, or a photo it can't re-encode, keeps whatever is embedded in it". The Hosting paragraph still
says "Photos are re-encoded in your browser before upload, which removes camera metadata such as location. PDFs, and
HEIC files your browser can't convert, are stored as uploaded." Two versions of one fact on a legal page invite the
question of which is true.
**Fix:** keep it in Receipts only, and cut the Hosting paragraph to
`Hosting, database and file storage providers store your account details, receipt data and files on our behalf.`
Tighten the Receipts sentence to:
`We store your files, and send them to the AI provider, in the form your browser uploads them. Your browser re-encodes most photos, which removes details such as the camera model and location. A PDF, or a photo your browser can't re-encode, keeps whatever details are embedded in it.`

**P2: one record, two field lists.** In What we collect, the confirm-it's-you record holds "when, how …, what it was
for and the IP address". In How long we keep it, it holds "the method, the outcome and the IP address".
**Fix:** use one list in both places: `when, how (passkey, app code, backup code or emailed code), what it was for,
whether it worked, and the IP address`. The retention bullet can then say `The record of each time you confirmed it's
you is kept for 90 days, to spot attempts to break in.`

**P3: the retention section is now eleven paragraphs in two styles.** The new paragraphs mix a label
("Sign-in records: …") with plain sentences ("A workspace invitation is deleted …", "We keep a log …"). At 390 the
page is about 8,600 px tall and this section has no handholds.
**Fix:** start every paragraph in How long we keep it with a label, the way What we collect does. Use
`Account and receipts:`, `Support messages:`, `Subscription records:`, `Usage records:`, `Sign-in records:`,
`Invitations:`, `Background tasks:` and `Administrator log:`. Merge the three short receipt and account-deletion
paragraphs under the first label. Keep body text and type as they are; the labels are enough.

**P4: the admin log doesn't say what account deletion does to it.** Every other retention paragraph covers deletion.
**Fix:** add the true one of `It is kept for 2 years even after you delete your account.` or
`Entries about you are deleted with your account.` Check `admin_audit_log`'s foreign keys to choose.

**P5: the date format differs from the app's.** The header reads "Last updated November 14, 2026". The app writes
"1 December" and "17 Nov 2026".
**Fix:** `Last updated 14 November 2026`. Set `LEGAL_UPDATED.privacy` to the day the page ships, not the day the PR
was written.

### #278 (shipped): review statuses and duplicate chips

| #   | Severity   | Screen and state                                  | Screenshot                                                      |
| --- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R1  | Should-fix | Receipts list, rows with two flags, 1440 and 390  | `app-receipts@1440-dark.png`, `app-receipts@390-light.png`      |
| R2  | Should-fix | Receipts filters                                  | `app-receipts@1440-dark.png`                                    |
| R3  | Nit        | Receipts list, phone rows                         | `app-receipts@390-light.png`                                    |
| R4  | Nit        | Receipt detail, duplicate panel                   | `app-receipts-348b3c96-…@1440-light.png`                        |

**R1: two amber chips per row turn the column into a wall of amber.** A possible duplicate always also needs review,
so most flagged rows carry `Needs review` and `Possible duplicate` stacked, both in the warning tone. The second chip
doubles the row height at 1440 and adds a line at 390, and it says nothing the first doesn't, except why.
**Fix:** show one chip per row, the most specific reason: `Possible duplicate` outranks the generic `Needs review`.
List the other reasons in the chip's tooltip and accessible name, for example `Possible duplicate; also needs review`.
The detail page keeps both, where there is room.

**R2: "Needs review" and "Not reviewed" sit side by side.** A reader can't tell the difference from the labels.
**Fix (owner decision D4):** rename `Needs review` to `Flagged`, and keep `Not reviewed` for every receipt nobody has
confirmed yet.

**R3: phone rows lose the category when they have a flag.** Rows marked Reviewed show `2 Oct 2026 · Fuel`. Flagged
rows show the date alone, with the chips right-aligned on a line of their own.
**Fix:** keep `date · category` on every row, and left-align the chips under it, in line with the merchant.

**R4: the duplicate panel names both rules, not the one that matched.** It says "A receipt added earlier has the same
bill number and seller, or the same merchant, date and total."
**Fix:** say which rule matched:
`Matches a receipt added 17 Nov 2026, 10:05: same merchant, date and total. Check it isn't counted twice.`, or
`… same bill number and seller. …`

## Accessibility notes

- Focus rings: the card's links use `focus-visible:ring-2 ring-ring`, and the `UpgradeTrigger` and `ContactButton`
  variants come from `packages/ui`. No keyboard walk was done in this round.
- Contrast wasn't measured with a tool this round. By eye, the riskiest pair is `text-xs text-muted-foreground` on
  the card in light theme (`Ask the owner for Pro.`, `pr-290/member-1440-light.png`). Check it against 4.5:1 in the
  #290 fix round.
- Tap targets: the phone variants of the card's actions are 44 px in code. The strip's `Upgrade >` is 44 px tall but
  about 70 px wide, which is fine.
- No horizontal page scroll at 390 in any combined capture. The receipts filter row scrolls inside its own container,
  with the last chip cut at the edge (`Reviewe…`), and that cut is the only cue that it scrolls. A trailing fade
  would make the overflow obvious.
